# ST01 Filling Station Equipment Guide

## Scope and document control
Document ID: EQ-ST01-001. Revision: 1. Effective date: 2026-10-24. Owner: Kelvara Maintenance Engineering. Applies to ST01_FILLING. Fictional equipment details and synthetic training instructions; not manufacturer instructions. Existing iMAKS limits are retained. No intrusive repair or isolation sequence is supplied.

## Equipment and process path
ST01_FILLING: For PO-1001, the fictional product path is inlet valve VLV-ST01-01, filter FLT-ST01-01, pump PMP-ST01-01, and filling manifold MAN-ST01-01. ST01_FILLING_PRS, model PRS-4410, measures delivery pressure downstream of the pump. ST01_FILLING_FLW, model FLW-4450, measures delivered flow. The instrumentation measures process delivery pressure, not a pneumatic supply. No upstream-pressure sensor or filter differential-pressure measurement is supplied.

## Operator observation points
ST01_FILLING: HMI-ST01 outside the closed guard provides Diagnostics, Alarm History, Stop, and Alarm Acknowledge. OBS-ST01-01 is the external inspection window facing the inlet valve position indicator and pump status lamp. The valve OPEN/CLOSED/UNKNOWN indicator can be read without access. If obscured, record not visible. Do not infer valve position from pressure. The emergency-stop control is beside HMI-ST01.

## Lone-worker scope and tools
ST01_FILLING: The trained operator may read the HMI, command normal Stop, record evidence, and observe the external indicators without tools. Do not operate the process valve, loosen fittings, drain lines, open filters, prime the pump, remove guards, or reset motor protection. Those actions require a qualified process-maintenance technician and separate approved instructions for isolation, depressurization, and hygiene. A stopped pump does not prove pressure has been removed. Use RES-ST01-001 for the incident-specific assistance route.
