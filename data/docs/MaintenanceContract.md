# Maintenance Contract MC-2026-01

Between Kelvara Packaging GmbH and a fictional service provider. Effective 2026-01-01. Fictional document.

## 1. Scope
The service provider performs the predictive maintenance actions MAINT-01 and MAINT-02 on ST04_PACKAGING and ST02_SEALING, as defined in SOP-003.

## 2. Response Times
For CRITICAL alarms at ST02_SEALING the provider is on site within 4 hours. For HIGH priority actions such as MAINT-02 (sealing jaw inspection) the provider responds within 2 working days.

## 3. Spare Parts
Kelvara keeps a stock of TMP-2210 sensors and sealing jaw elements. Spare sensors are ordered from ThermoSense GmbH.
