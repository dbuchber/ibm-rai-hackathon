# ST02 Installed Sensor and Protection Gap

## Scope and document control
Document ID: SPEC-ST02-001. Revision: 1. Effective date: 2026-10-24. Owner: Kelvara Maintenance Engineering. Applies to ST02_SEALING. Fictional equipment details and synthetic training instructions; not manufacturer instructions. Existing iMAKS limits are retained. No intrusive repair or isolation sequence is supplied.

## Applicable sources
ST02_SEALING: The installed ST02_SEALING_TMP model is TMP-2210 per EQ-ST02-001. DS-THERM-2200 specifies 200 degrees C continuous maximum and 220 degrees C intermittent for less than ten minutes, not for control. SOP-001 RULE-ST02-01 gives an operating band of 175 to 195 degrees C; RULE-ST02-02 and SOP-002 give a critical threshold above 210 degrees C. The three numbers have different meanings and must be cited separately.

## Protection gap and decision
ST02_SEALING: A 210 degrees C critical threshold does not make continuous operation up to 210 degrees C acceptable for TMP-2210. At the replay peak of 205 degrees C, the approved operating band and the continuous instrument rating are already exceeded although the critical threshold has not been crossed. Surface this protection mismatch rather than treating absence of a critical alarm as permission to continue. The intermittent 220 degrees C note cannot authorize process control at 205 degrees C.

## Revision and applicability
ST02_SEALING: This synthetic applicability note is revision 1, effective 2026-10-24. It records the mismatch; it does not change SOP-001, SOP-002, or DS-THERM-2200, approve a new threshold, or certify a sensor replacement. No approved engineering change, installed replacement, independent reference measurement, or revised control configuration is included for INC-ST02-20261024-01. Any proposed higher-rated model in the graph must not be treated as installed equipment.
