# ST02 Sealing Station Equipment Guide

## Scope and document control
Document ID: EQ-ST02-001. Revision: 1. Effective date: 2026-10-24. Owner: Kelvara Maintenance Engineering. Applies to ST02_SEALING. Fictional equipment details and synthetic training instructions; not manufacturer instructions. Existing iMAKS limits are retained. No intrusive repair or isolation sequence is supplied.

## Installed equipment
ST02_SEALING: Sealing jaw JAW-ST02-01 and heater HTR-ST02-01 are inside a fixed guard. Temperature sensor ST02_SEALING_TMP is the installed ThermoSense TMP-2210, as recorded in this scenario installation register. ST02_SEALING_CUR measures motor current. HMI-ST02 is outside the guard at the operator position. Production order PO-1003 is affected. The replacement Series 2400 mentioned in DS-THERM-2200 is a recommendation, not the installed sensor.

## Controls and observation point
ST02_SEALING: HMI-ST02 provides Diagnostics, Alarm History, Stop, and Alarm Acknowledge. OBS-ST02-01 is the marked position outside the closed guard facing the jaw inspection window. Read temperatures from HMI-ST02; do not touch the jaw or sensor, and do not open guards. An emergency-stop control is next to the panel. Acknowledge records receipt; it does not reset a trip or authorize restart.

## Lone-worker scope
ST02_SEALING: The trained line operator may read diagnostics, stop the station with the documented Stop control, record evidence, and observe from OBS-ST02-01. Sensor replacement, parameter edits, electrical measurements, heater adjustment, and guarded access require a qualified technician and the separate approved isolation instruction. A stopped machine can remain hot and energized. Contact fictional Maintenance Desk extension 240; if unavailable, retain the stop and log the attempt.
