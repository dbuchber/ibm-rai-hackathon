# ST02 Temperature Recovery and Restart Conditions

## Scope and document control
Document ID: REC-ST02-001. Revision: 1. Effective date: 2026-10-24. Owner: Kelvara Maintenance Engineering. Applies to ST02_SEALING. Fictional equipment details and synthetic training instructions; not manufacturer instructions. Existing iMAKS limits are retained. No intrusive repair or isolation sequence is supplied.

## Hold and qualified review
ST02_SEALING: After the reported excursion above the TMP-2210 continuous rating, retain the stop pending qualified maintenance assessment and engineering review of SPEC-ST02-001. The lone operator cannot authorize a test, modify thresholds, or approve a replacement model. Cooling below a limit does not establish instrument accuracy or resolve the protection mismatch.

## Prerequisites for verification
ST02_SEALING: A qualified technician must document the cause assessment, independent temperature verification using the separate approved measurement instruction, any corrective work, and completion of the approved isolation/restoration process. Engineering must record an approved disposition of the protection mismatch, including applicable instrument, operating limits, and controller protection. The shift supervisor must authorize a controlled verification test; all guards and protective functions must be restored. The physical repair, test-start, and reset sequences are outside this dataset.

## Verification and release
ST02_SEALING: For the fictional supervised verification at a target of 185 degrees C, record good-quality control readings and an independent reference at intervals no greater than ten seconds for five uninterrupted minutes. Both must stay within 183.8 to 186.2 degrees C, and their difference must not exceed the DS-THERM-2200 accuracy of 1.5 degrees C. An invalid sample, missing reference, alarm, or failed criterion returns the station to maintenance. These acceptance criteria do not resolve the protection mismatch by themselves. Explicit alarm clearance, qualified maintenance confirmation, engineering disposition, and supervisor production release are required. None is supplied for this incident.
