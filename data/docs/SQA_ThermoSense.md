# Supplier Quality Agreement SQA-THERMO-001

Between Kelvara Packaging GmbH (Buyer) and ThermoSense GmbH (Supplier). Effective 2026-01-01. Fictional document.

## 1. Scope
ThermoSense GmbH supplies the temperature sensor TMP-2210 and the humidity sensor HUM-2250 for iMAKS line A. The products conform to datasheet DS-THERM-2200.

## 2. Warranty Conditions
The warranty for TMP-2210 covers continuous operation up to 200°C, as stated in DS-THERM-2200. Operation above 200°C voids the warranty for drift caused by thermocouple ageing. The Buyer is responsible for process limits at ST02_SEALING that respect this rating.

## 3. Calibration and Replacement
Sensors installed at ST02_SEALING and ST01_FILLING are calibrated every 12 months. ThermoSense replaces a defective TMP-2210 within 5 working days. Drift beyond ±5°C within 500 hours is a warranty case only if the sensor was operated within its rating.
